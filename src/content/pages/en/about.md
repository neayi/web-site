Neayi combines a digital culture, change management methods and skills in software and digital marketing. The company runs Triple Performance, builds Itinera and supports agricultural organisations in their transition projects. It is based in Toulouse, France, and is a member of the [Cofarming](https://cofarming.info) network and of the [OSFarm](https://osfarm.org) association.

## Our values

- **Collaborative**: everyone has something to contribute, a practice, a way of working, a validation.
- **Open**: knowledge should not stay hidden; the platform is open to all.
- **Dynamic**: data shows where and how practices spread, reassuring farmers in their choices.
- **Inclusive**: organic, conventional, conservation agriculture, newcomers, researchers; dialogue drives innovation.
