Triple Performance brings together about **8,000 freely accessible pages**: 629 farmer case studies, 803 technical sheets on crops, pests, beneficial organisms and equipment, thematic portals and Q&As. More than **60,000 people** read it every month, mostly arriving from search engines.

The platform is available in 11 languages: French, English, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Dutch, Greek, Finnish, Hungarian, Italian and Polish.

## A commons, run by Neayi

Like Wikipedia, Triple Performance belongs to the people who keep it alive. Neayi is the organisation behind it: the team maintains the commons so that it stays reliable, visible and useful.

- **Content structure**: menus, portals, keywords and categories, reviewed regularly with experts.
- **Thematic dossiers**: spotting new topics, literature research, user votes.
- **Search visibility**: so that articles rank first and reach farmers.
- **Community work**: active in more than 300 Facebook and WhatsApp groups of farmers and advisers.
- **Technical maintenance** and **usability improvements**, built with the community.

## The ecosystem

We do not build Triple Performance alone. The project relies on content partners, schools and universities, research projects, funders and associations.
