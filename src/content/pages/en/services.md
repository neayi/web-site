## Editorial engineering

We turn your specialists' expertise into articles and thematic dossiers published on Triple Performance. After an interview and literature research, we map the key concepts with the expert, then write: farmer case studies, charts, videos, illustrations. You can reuse the content in your own communication, and your logo appears as a contributor.

## Transforming farming models

We facilitate collective work on a specific transition. With the Var Chamber of Agriculture, for example, we worked on disease-resistant grape varieties: identifying regulatory and technical barriers, running workshops, interviewing winegrowers, advisers and decision-makers.

## Knowledge outreach and community

We take your content to where farmers are: search engines, more than 300 Facebook and WhatsApp groups, professional networks. We answer questions, defuse controversies and escalate complex cases to the expert.

## Data and integration

We combine, synchronise and integrate knowledge bases through APIs, Excel, XML or JSON. Do you have a list of concepts (grape varieties, cover crops, fertilisers)? We can generate pages with a dedicated search engine, set up an integration workflow, and more. Don't stop at publishing an Excel file!

## Software development

We are experts in knowledge-focused software engineering. We can help you share your data, knowledge and expertise beyond a simple PDF posted online: interactive decision support tools, APIs, open data, AI… Call us to imagine new ways of supporting farmers through their transition together.
