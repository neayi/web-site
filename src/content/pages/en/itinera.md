Itinera, built by Neayi, is a collaborative web platform that maps crop management plans visually and calculates their technical, economic and environmental performance.

*Itinera is currently available in French only.*

## What AI brings

- **Assisted input**: describe your system in a few sentences and Itinera fills in the crop management plan, which you then adjust.
- **System analysis**: ask questions about a rotation and the AI answers from the system's data.
- **More than 40 indicators** calculated automatically, to compare variants: a new rotation, another variety, cover crops, reduced tillage… Every seed, input and machine is identified in our reference databases, and cost, ecotoxicity and greenhouse gas data are calculated and tracked over time.

## Who is it for?

Farmers, advisers, training organisations, research projects, supply chains and farm support organisations.

## How it connects with Triple Performance

Systems simulated in Itinera are geolocated and, if you make them public, can feed a reference corpus adapted to each territory. Triple Performance case studies can be used as a starting point for a model.
